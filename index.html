Book cleaning, order food, request a ride and explore news and sports coverage — all from one place.

Customer service portal

Choose a service

🧹 Cleaning request

Submit your details to request a cleaning service. Price and availability must be confirmed.

🍔 Food delivery

Choose a participating store and send the order request. The store must confirm availability, total price and delivery.

🚗 Request a ride

Enter pickup and destination. Route estimates depend on internet access and the public routing service.

Enter pickup and destination, then select Estimate route.

A route estimate is not a confirmed fare. Confirm driver availability and the price before travelling.
